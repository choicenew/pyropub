Privacy Policy

VehereGo Mobile Application

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how oakgeol ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our") processes, handles, and protects your information when you use the VehereGo mobile application (the "Application").

1. Overview & 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture

VehereGo is engineered with a local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. The Application provides status bar Live Activity capsules, parcel pickup code OCR recognition, Apple Wallet (.pkpass) ticket management, and home screen Glance widgets.

All core operations—including optical character recognition (OCR) of parcel codes, parsing of incoming notifications for live activity status, widget rendering, and PKPass file parsing—are executed 100% locally on your mobile device. We do not require user accounts, nor do we upload, transmit, or store your personal data, parcel codes, travel vouchers, or notification contents to external cloud servers.

2. Information Collection and Use

Because VehereGo processes data locally on your device, we do not collect personal identification information directly. The Application stores settings, imported vouchers, and parsed pickup items solely in local system storage (such as Android SharedPreferences and local SQLite/Room databases).

3. Android System Permissions & Purpose

To deliver its core utility, the Application may request the following system permissions on your device:

  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): Required exclusively to detect and parse incoming parcel, logistics, and ride status updates locally to display status bar Live Activity capsules. Notification contents are processed entirely in memory and on-device.
  • System Overlay / Appear on Top (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Required to render status bar live activity capsules and floating HUD controls on top of other applications.
  • Storage / Media / File Access: Required to allow users to manually select and import Apple Wallet (.pkpass) ticket files or images containing parcel codes.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to display status bar live activity alerts and widget update notifications.

All permission data remains strictly within your device environment and is never shared or exported by the Service Provider.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes third-party software development kits (SDKs) and infrastructure services to maintain app stability, monitor performance, and provide system functionality. These third parties may collect device information in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Unity

5. Log Data and Diagnostics

In the event of an application error or crash, diagnostic Log Data may be automatically collected via third-party services such as Firebase Crashlytics. This data may include your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model name, operating system version, application configuration, time and date of usage, and crash diagnostic logs. This information is used strictly to diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Since all personal data, parcel records, and PKPass tickets reside exclusively on your local device storage, you retain full control over your data. You may delete all Application data at any time by:

  • Clearing the Application's storage and cache via Android Settings > Apps > VehereGo > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Application from your device, which automatically removes all locally stored application files.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary action.

8. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. We leverage native Android sandboxing, local encryption mechanisms, and permission controls to safeguard your data locally on your device. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Because VehereGo does not store personal data on remote servers or maintain user profiles, your data remains fully under your local control.
