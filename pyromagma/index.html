❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Key questions and answers regarding PyroMagma AI Academic Reader

Q: How do PyroMagma's AI paper breakdowns and RAG Q&A work?

When opening a paper, PyroMagma builds a local vector index of the text. The LLM extracts key innovations and enables interactive RAG Q&A pinned directly to exact paper sections and mathematical equations.

Q: Will PyroMagma expose my research direction or history?

Absolutely not. PyroMagma uses 100% on-device algorithms for recommendations and processing. Your reading history, bookmarks, and search terms stay strictly on your device.

Q: Which document formats are supported?

Native support for PDF papers, EPUB ebooks, and RSS academic subscription feeds, with direct searching and reading from ArXiv and bioRxiv.
