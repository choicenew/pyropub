Privacy Policy

Pyro Magma Mobile Application

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how oakgeol ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our") processes, handles, and protects your information when you use the Pyro Magma mobile application (the "Application").

1. Overview & On-Device Processing Privacy

Pyro Magma is an AI-driven academic literature, reading, and research assistant application. It offers AI paper digests, literature RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), Zotero synchronization, and bilingual translation features.

The Application is designed with a privacy-first approach. Your reading history, bookmarked academic papers, research notes, local document indexing, and local AI recommendations are processed 100% locally on your mobile device. We do not track, collect, or upload your reading behavior or academic document history to cloud databases for advertising or user profiling.

2. Information Collection and Use

Because Pyro Magma processes research data on-device, we do not require user account registration nor do we collect personal identity information. Local preferences, document indexes, and reading progress are stored strictly in local device storage.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates third-party services for app infrastructure, performance monitoring, crash diagnostics, and optional ad delivery. These service providers may collect information used to identify your device in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Unity

4. Log Data and Crash Diagnostics

If the Application encounters an error or crash during use, diagnostic Log Data may be generated and collected via automated crash-reporting tools such as Firebase Crashlytics. This data includes device IP address, device hardware model, operating system version, app state at the time of the crash, and diagnostic information needed to fix bugs and improve stability.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features & Data Privacy

The Application incorporates edge AI algorithms and optional translation engines to deliver literature summaries and translations. AI processing of local documents occurs directly on your device hardware. Any optional cloud translation requests are executed solely to deliver requested translation results and are not used to build user profiles.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Policy

All reading data, cached research documents, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear or remove all application data at any time by:

  • Clearing local application data via Android Settings > Apps > Pyro Magma > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the Application from your device, which immediately removes all stored application files.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages.

8. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. We employ native Android security standards, local file isolation, and system permission safeguards to keep your research data protected on your device. However, please be aware that no electronic storage method is completely infallible.

9. Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA & DSA)

Under regulations such as GDPR, CCPA, and the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), users have rights regarding their personal data. Because Pyro Magma does not collect personal data or host remote user profiles, your data remains in your control on your device. For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at OAKGEOL@GMAIL.COM.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page regularly for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
